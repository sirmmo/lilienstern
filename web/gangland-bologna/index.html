Gangland: Bologna

Run a gang in Bologna, block by block.

The city is cut into its real census sections (or its 90 statistical areas), with each block's wealth from the median income of its area and its residents from the city's open data. The gangs start in the poorer blocks: they deal, extort and recruit, raise crews and send them across the city, and learn the tricks of the trade, while the police patrols where the violence is. You run one gang; the Lilienstern engine runs the others and the police, in your browser.

How to play

  1. Click a block to see who holds it, how strong each gang is there, its wealth, police, heat and violence, the crews in it, and the moves you can make there.
  2. You have three moves a day. Move in next to your turf, reinforce and hold what you have, attack a rival's block, open corners, rackets, labs and safehouses. Moves cost crew, which your safehouses recruit in poor blocks, or cash, which your corners (supplied by your labs) and rackets (in rich blocks) make.
  3. Crews: raise street soldiers, shooters or enforcers on a block you hold, then click one of your crews (on the map, or in a block's card) and click where it should go. Crews that meet a rival's fight, every day, until one side is gone; a crew standing on a block with no rival crew takes it over, day by day. Crews cost cash every day: run out, and they lose health.
  4. Contacts come from the street, your corners and your rackets, and go into what you learn: click Choose what to research. Scooters bring fast riders, contract killers bring hitmen, and the rest make your crews stronger, faster or cheaper.
  5. End the day (space), or let the days run, and the city moves: rivals expand and fight, the police patrols where the violence is and raids busy blocks.
  6. A block is yours while you are the strongest there by a margin. Lose it, and what you built there goes to the new holder. The game lasts two years: hold as much of the city as you can.
  7. With friends: on one device, choose every gang a person plays and take turns; online, open a room and send its code.

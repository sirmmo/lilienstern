Gangland: Bologna

Run a gang in Bologna, block by block.

The city is cut into its real census sections (or its 90 statistical areas), with each block's wealth from the median income of its area and its residents from the city's open data. Six gangs start in the poorer blocks. You run one; the others and the police are run by the Lilienstern engine, in your browser.

How to play

  1. Click a block to see who holds it, how strong each gang is there, its wealth, police and violence, and the moves you can make there.
  2. You have three moves a day. Move in next to your turf, reinforce and hold what you have, attack a rival's block. Moves cost crew, which your safehouses recruit in poor blocks, or cash, which your corners (supplied by your labs) and rackets (in rich blocks) make.
  3. End the day (space) and the city moves: rivals expand and attack, gangs clash where they meet, the police patrols where the violence is and raids busy blocks.
  4. A block is yours while you are the strongest there by a margin. Lose it, and what you built there goes to the new holder.
  5. The game lasts two years. Hold as much of the city as you can.
